# Forest Football 11 art

`players.png` is an original transparent 4×4 sprite atlas created with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-30. The original image is saved without bitmap edits; Godot selects directional animation frames and scans the alpha channel to align each frame's feet to the pitch.

`forest.png` reuses the generated forest atlas from `fighter/assets/forest.png`; its prompt and provenance are recorded in `fighter/assets/ART.md`. Pitch, stands, goals, nets, ball and shadows are real Godot 3D geometry. Ground markings and grass stripes are procedural. Noto Sans KR font licensing is in `soccer/OFL.txt`.
