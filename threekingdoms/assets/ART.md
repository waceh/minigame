# Three Kingdoms RTS artwork

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-31. These are original sprites; no Warcraft assets are included. Generated images retain their original RGBA transparency and resolution without raster editing. Godot selects atlas frames and mirrors them toward movement/attack directions.

Each character row has idle, walk left leg, walk right leg, attack. Godot uses normalized equal cells despite the generated dimensions not being exact multiples of the requested grid. Scholar, armored warrior and enemy silhouettes stay separate. Terrain, creek, bridge, gate, watchtowers, flags, lighting, shadows, selection rings and combat particles are Godot geometry/shaders.
