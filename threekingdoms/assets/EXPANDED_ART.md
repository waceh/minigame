# Expanded roster artwork

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-31. All final PNG files are saved in this assets directory with their source pixels intact. Runtime frame regions, alpha-component bounds and foot anchors are recorded in `../scripts/frames.gd`; Godot AtlasTexture regions keep neighboring sprites out of a frame. Linear mipmap filtering and alpha-to-coverage reduce edge shimmer. The source PNGs were not resized, painted or cut into replacement raster files.

The three base atlases use four columns by six rows, the recruits use four columns by three rows. Column order: idle, left walk step, right walk step, attack. Oak is one complete tree with no atlas boundary.
